Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about MNG and library makerspaces. Select a question to expand the answer.

  • What is the Makerspace Networking Group?

    The Makerspace Networking Group (MNG) is an informal network of library makerspace staff based in Illinois. Our goal is to share resources, lesson plans, equipment knowledge, and professional support across member libraries.

  • Who can join MNG?

    MNG is open to any library staff in Illinois who work with or are interested in makerspaces. This includes public, academic, school, and special libraries. There is no cost to participate.

  • How do I join the MNG Discord?

    Visit the Resources page for the Discord invite link. Once you join, introduce yourself in the #introductions channel and let us know which library you're from.

  • When do MNG meetings take place?

    Check the Meetings page for the current schedule. Meetings are held virtually and meeting links are posted in the MNG Discord.

  • How do I contribute a lesson plan or equipment guide?

    Share your materials in the MNG Discord or contact a group organizer. Once reviewed, it can be added to the Lesson Plans or Equipment Guides pages on this site.

  • How do I update or correct information on this site?

    This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Any MNG staff member with access to the repository can edit the HTML files directly in the GitHub web interface — no coding experience required. Contact your site administrator for access.
